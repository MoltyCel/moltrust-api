## Description:

Mint a MolTrust identity, bind a wallet to it by signature, and read a trust score. The private key is generated locally and never leaves the machine; nothing registers unless you ask for it.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

An agent developer who wants a verifiable identity for an agent runs `register` once, keeps the printed key, and binds the agent's wallet. Endpoints that price by identity can then charge the agent less than an anonymous caller — on the MolTrust gate that is 0.04 USDC against 0.05, a 20 % difference — and any third party can read the agent's score without an account.
